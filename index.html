Speak the language

A short glossary

ARV
After Repair Value — the estimated value of a property once renovations are complete.
DSCR
Debt Service Coverage Ratio — measures whether a property's income covers its own mortgage payment.
LTV
Loan-to-Value — the loan amount compared to the appraised value of the property.
Bridge Loan
Short-term financing that bridges the gap between buying a new property and selling an existing one.
Cap Rate
Net operating income divided by purchase price — a quick read on potential return.
Clear to Close (CTC)
The lender has signed off — the transaction is ready to close.
